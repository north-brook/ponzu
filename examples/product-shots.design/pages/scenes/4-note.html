Scenes

Each artboard is plain HTML in its finished state, plus a <script type="ponzu/act"> that plays it: the cursor moves, clicks and types, rows open, totals roll. The canvas loops it; a screenshot shows its poster; record films one frame per beat.

1 Invite is a corner-bleed hero beside copy: one gutter on top and left, the window running off right and bottom, real words only on the field, the button, the new member and the seats.

2 Settle up is a phone pinned from the top on a colored ground, with a touch cursor: the button changes state on itself and the balance rolls only its changed digits.

3 Answer streams a reply in place, sources last. No sparkles, no purple: it shows what the assistant does.

The judgment is adapted from Stageshow by Benji Taylor. Agents: get_guide('product'), get_guide('kit'), get_guide('act').

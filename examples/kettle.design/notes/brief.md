# Kettle: brief

**Concept.** Kettle is a shared household app for people who live together: roommates, couples, families. Chores that rotate fairly, one grocery list, bills split and settled, and a light household feed. Named for the thing that gets put on when everyone's home: the small ritual that makes a flat feel like a household.

**Tagline.** *Living together, sorted.*

**Personality.** The housemate who remembers whose turn it is without making it weird. Warm, dry, a little cheeky, never naggy. Kettle nudges; it never scolds.

## Palette (kitchen colors)
- Tomato `#E4572E`: primary. Buttons, the mark, the app icon.
- Iron `#2B1F1B`: ink. Text, dark surfaces (cast-iron pan, not black).
- Oat `#F5EDE0` / Paper `#FFFAF2`: backgrounds. Never pure white canvases.
- Butter `#F6C445` (Honey `#C38A0C` for lines and text): chores, streaks, celebration.
- Mint `#A9DBBE` / Basil `#2E7650`: groceries, "settled", success.
- Lilac `#D9CCF4` / Plum `#6B4D8F`: bills and money.
- Sky `#BFDDF2` / Navy `#24435C`: feed, info, system.
Each feature owns a color, so the product color-codes itself. Each housemate has a color too: Maya tomato-soft, Theo mint, Priya lilac, Ade sky.

## Type
- Display: **Fraunces** 600–700, tracked −3 to −5%. Italic for asides ("*sorted.*").
- Text: **DM Sans** 400–700.
- Mono: **Azeret Mono**, for architecture labels only.
- Scale: 88 / 56 / 34 / 22 / 17 / 15 / 13.

## Shape and space
- 4pt spacing. Generous radii (16–24 on cards, 32 on marketing cards), pills for chips and buttons.
- Avatars are colored circles with initials.
- Soft warm shadows (iron-tinted), never gray.

## Voice
- Plain, short, human. "Ade's on bins this week." not "Task assigned."
- Money is neutral: someone is "up", nobody "owes".
- Celebrate small: "Kitchen's done. Gold star, Theo."
- One exclamation mark per screen, at most.

Checkout errors after a payment timeout was lowered in config

Author Priya RamanReviewers Lena Hoffmann, Aiko TanakaReview meeting Thu 22 Oct, 16:00 UTC
Drafted by Halon Copilot from 61 timeline events and 214 Slack messages. Edited by Priya Raman.

Summary

At 13:58 UTC, checkout-api v4.18.2 shipped a config change that lowered PAYMENT_TIMEOUT_MS from 2500 to 800. Card authorizations routed through our Adyen fallback take 900–1400 ms at p95, so they began timing out. 4.1% of checkout attempts failed for 19 minutes until the release was rolled back.

Failed checkouts18,412
Merchants affected2,906
Time to mitigate19m 07s
Error budget used41%

Contributing factors

1The timeout was tuned against primary-processor latency only. Nothing in the change review showed fallback-route latency.
2Config-only releases skip the canary stage because the skip_canary flag is set at the org level.
3Fallback traffic is 6% of volume, so the error-rate alert crossed 2% four minutes after deploy instead of immediately.

What went well

Marcus acknowledged in 89 seconds and the rollback took under three minutes once the cause was linked. The status page was updated seven minutes after the first alert, before any merchant ticket arrived.

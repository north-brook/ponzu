# Product shots

1. `get_guide('product')`, `get_guide('kit')` and `get_guide('act')` if you haven't this session. Product is the judgment, kit the parts at real geometry, act the scene language.
2. Plan before any HTML, in a "Note: …" artboard beside the work: where it sits on the page, the focal object, the action, the payoff, and what to leave out. Count the real strings (usually 3 to 7).
3. With the product's code or site to hand, take its tokens, icons, chrome and words (the `ponzu-code-to-design` skill). Otherwise light, one accent, and the brief's style in full.
4. `create_artboard` at the size it's shown, 1:1 (about 720×340 beside copy, 960–1100 wide full-width, 400×300 for a card). Put the picture in a clipping stage, pick a layout, and build the screen from the kit's window, phone, rows and bars.
5. Screenshot at scale 1 and frame it before any motion: the hero runs off an edge, one gutter, words only where the eye lands, no dead corners.
6. Tell the story with a `<script type="ponzu/act">` scene: three to five beats, a payoff that holds, `poster` on the frame that tells it, `reset` with a cause.
7. Screenshot (the poster) and `record` (the storyboard: one frame per beat). Review them as a stranger would, using the questions in the product guide, and fix the largest fault first: framing, then hierarchy, then motion, then polish.
8. Point the person at it with the Link from `get_basic_info`, then watch the canvas for their comments (the `ponzu-watch` skill) instead of ending your turn.

For a set (a bento grid, a page of demos), plan the tile list first and keep one figure language: one ground, one gutter, one window shadow.

Billing on Ponzu starts on February 1

Until then, Ponzu stays free, and nothing is charged until an admin adds a card.

From then, each workspace gets 100 free credits for each member each month. Past those, credits cost $0.10 each and need a card, which an admin adds. Each workspace has a monthly cap, $100 unless an admin changes it, and Ponzu never bills past it.

See the pricing https://ponzu.design/pricing

You get this because Ponzu's terms promise it by email. It can't be turned off.

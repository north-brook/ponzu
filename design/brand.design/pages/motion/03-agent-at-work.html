The develop

looped here · once per write

What an agent writes doesn't pop in or slide. A beat after it lands, each new piece shows its plan, yuzu outlines round it and the first few boxes inside it, while the piece itself is hidden; then it develops top first as the outlines fade. A new board draws its frame first. Nothing moves: the layout is where it stays from the first frame. Your own edits, undo and redo just appear.

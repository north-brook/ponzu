The slice

A citrus slice cut straight through: six segments, no rind, one color. It reads as fruit first, and as an asterisk or a spinner second, which is why it can also be Ponzu's loader: it turns a sixth and rests, and every rest is the mark again.

Construction · 64-unit grid

Built from one rule

Each segment is a 60° sector of a 27.3-unit circle, inset 4 units from its neighbours and drawn with a 3.4-unit round stroke in its own color. The stroke rounds the corners and closes the gaps to 4.6. Draw it from SLICE; never retrace it.

Grid64 × 64 units
Segments6 × 60°, gap on the vertical
Outer radius29 (27.3 sector + 1.7 stroke)
Gap4.6
Cornersround join, 3.4 stroke
Colorone: ink, yuzu or paper

HOW IT WOULD WORK
NoticingEach Mac already checks GitHub Releases on launch and every four hours, and downloads in the background. Its version joins what it shares with your other Macs, so one check is enough for all of them, and the button shows on each. The menu’s Check for Updates stays.
Updating the othersOut of sight: clicking Update asks each Mac, over the same tailnet relay that opens their designs, to install and restart. Agents ride through the restart: their bridge reconnects and resends any call that never arrived. An offline Mac updates when it’s back, and this one restarts last.
Where the button livesThe title bar, beside the agents, because it’s on screen in every tab and on the home screen. Yuzu is the brand’s one accent, so it reads as new without a badge or a dialog. No more sheet asking you to restart.

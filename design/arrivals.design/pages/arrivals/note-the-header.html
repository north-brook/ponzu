What Paper puts in the header
No avatar. To the right of the artboard's name, Paper draws a rain: 32 columns of tiny dots in the agent's color, two rows deep. Each column falls at its own speed, and the speed drifts on a slow sine. The columns appear one by one over 400ms as the agent starts, and fall away when it stops. The rain always starts after the label, so it never runs over the name.
Who is working lives in presence and the tooltip, not on the board.
G, rain. Paper's idea in yuzu. It has the most life, and it reads as "busy" at a glance from far out.
H, slice. Our loader with a one-word verb taken from set_status. It's the most on-brand, and the verb says something useful.
I, the name glows. Adds nothing. The name already shimmers today; this warms it to yuzu and adds a live dot. It's the calmest, and it pairs best with the ring.
My pick: I. With the ring breathing around the board, one more moving thing in the header is a lot. G is right if you want the header itself to feel alive.

The artboard and its header
Today a new board fades up from 98.5% scale over 420ms, and its name pops in. All three directions below keep C for the content and replace that fade.
Shared by all three. The agent's mark lands first with a small overshoot, because it's who's arriving. The sheet develops out of the canvas's own color, light or dark, as Paper's veil does. The yuzu arrival line hands off to the existing working trace, so the board never has two yuzu outlines at once.
D, draw the frame. The most "magical" of the three: the agent appears to sketch the board's edge. The cost is that it adds a second yuzu line, briefly, before the trace starts.
E, header leads. Has the clearest cause and effect: the name lands and the sheet unrolls out of it. It's the only one with motion on the sheet itself, but it's a clip, so nothing shifts.
F, placeholder. The quietest and fastest to read: "this space is taken." It's also the most useful when a board is created empty and filled later, because the placeholder can stay until the first write.
My pick: D. It's the closest to Paper's "plan, then thing" at the board level, and the line reads as ours in yuzu.

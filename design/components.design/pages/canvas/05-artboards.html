Artboards

An artboard's label sits 22px above it, and its corners round 4px. Selection is ink. Yuzu appears only when an agent is working there: rain falls at the right of the label, and a ring breathes 4px off the board. A new board draws its frame first. Dragging on the open canvas selects, and opening a file turns the slice.

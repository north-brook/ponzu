Design cards

The home screen is a grid of cards, one per design: its canvas preview at 16:11, its name, when it changed, and where it lives: the project and the branch it's on, or once archived, why. The same design, unchanged on several branches, folds into one card that says how many. Right-click a card for its menu. While an agent works in a design, yuzu rain falls where its time would be, the same rain as a working artboard's header, and its name shimmers.

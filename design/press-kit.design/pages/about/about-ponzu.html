About Ponzu

In one line

Ponzu is the design canvas for your coding agent.

Boilerplate

Ponzu is an open-source design canvas for coding agents. Agents such as Claude Code, Codex and Cursor draw on an infinite canvas of artboards over MCP, writing plain HTML and CSS, and you watch it happen live. You steer with comments pinned to the canvas; the agent picks them up, makes the change and resolves them. Designs are .design folders of HTML files on your own disk, so you can commit them next to your code. Ponzu runs on your machine, needs no account, and is free under the MIT license.

Writing the name

Ponzu in sentences, capital P. The lowercase ponzu is the wordmark only, and ponzu is the command.
